# Awesome Ecommerce SEO [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of the best SEO tools & resources for **ecommerce and independent (DTC) stores** — Shopify, WooCommerce, and beyond.

</div>

Generic SEO lists are everywhere. This one is focused on the work online-store owners
actually do: making **product**, **collection**, and **content** pages rank — and, in the
AI-search era, making them **citable** by answer engines through clean structured data.

Every link is checked. Tools marked **`free`** have a usable free tier or are fully free.

## Site Audit & Crawlers

- [website-seo-audit](https://github.com/zxhydfzr/website-seo-audit) - `free` Zero-dependency auditor that crawls a site and grades on-page, technical, and JSON-LD structured-data signals. Runs as a CLI or an AI agent skill.
- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) - `free` Google's own tool to monitor indexing, queries, coverage, and technical issues.
- [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) - `free` Bing's equivalent, with keyword and crawl insights.
- [Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) - Desktop crawler that audits on-site SEO issues at scale (free up to 500 URLs).
- [Sitebulb](https://sitebulb.com/) - Website auditor with prioritized hints and crawl visualizations.
- [python-seo-analyzer](https://github.com/sethblack/python-seo-analyzer) - `free` Open-source Python crawler that flags common technical SEO issues.
- [SEOnaut](https://github.com/StJudeWasHere/seonaut) - `free` Open-source, self-hostable technical SEO auditing tool.

## Structured Data & Rich Results

The signals that win product rich results in Google — and increasingly, citations in AI answers.

- [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) - `free` Checks which rich results your structured data is eligible for.
- [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org/) - `free` Validates Schema.org JSON-LD / microdata syntax.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/) - `free` The vocabulary reference for `Product`, `Offer`, `Review`, `BreadcrumbList`, and more.

## Keyword & Market Research

- [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) - `free` Keyword volume and ideas straight from Google Ads.
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/) - `free` Compare search interest and spot seasonality for products.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) - Keyword, backlink, and competitor research suite.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) - All-in-one keyword, competitor, and site-audit platform.
- [Moz](https://moz.com/) - Keyword research, rank tracking, and link metrics (Domain Authority).
- [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - Budget-friendly keyword and content ideas.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com/) - Visualizes question-based search queries around a keyword.

## On-Page & Content

- [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/) - Content editor that scores drafts against the current SERP.
- [Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io/) - Content optimization by term coverage and relevance.
- [Frase](https://www.frase.io/) - AI-assisted content briefs and optimization.
- [Yoast SEO](https://yoast.com/) - On-page SEO guidance and previews (WordPress & Shopify).
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - `free` Readability checker for cleaner product and blog copy.

## Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals

- [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) - `free` Lab + field Core Web Vitals with concrete fixes.
- [Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview/) - `free` Open-source auditing for performance, SEO, and accessibility.
- [CompressFile Image Audit](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kfofpbpbbbhahiofnkejpfgbomnfeopf) - `free` Chrome extension that flags oversized and badly compressed images on product and category pages and compresses them in the browser.
- [web.dev](https://web.dev/) - `free` Google's guidance on Core Web Vitals and the modern web.
- [GTmetrix](https://gtmetrix.com/) - Page-speed analysis and scheduled monitoring.
- [WebPageTest](https://www.webpagetest.org/) - Deep waterfall performance testing from real locations.

## Shopify SEO Apps

- [Smart SEO](https://apps.shopify.com/smart-seo) - Auto meta tags, JSON-LD structured data, and sitemaps for Shopify.
- [SearchPie SEO](https://apps.shopify.com/seo-booster) - Speed, meta, schema, and broken-link fixes in one app.
- [Yoast SEO for Shopify](https://yoast.com/shopify/) - Yoast's on-page optimization brought to Shopify stores.

## WooCommerce & WordPress SEO

- [Yoast SEO](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-seo/) - `free` The classic WordPress/WooCommerce SEO plugin.
- [Rank Math](https://rankmath.com/) - `free` Feature-rich WordPress SEO plugin with built-in schema.
- [All in One SEO](https://aioseo.com/) - WordPress & WooCommerce SEO toolkit.

## AI Search / GEO

Generative Engine Optimization — showing up in AI answers, not just blue links. Clean
structured data (see above) is the foundation; these help you measure and monitor.

- [Otterly.ai](https://otterly.ai/) - Monitor how your brand and links appear in AI search answers.

- [AccuRanker](https://www.accuranker.com/) - Fast, accurate keyword rank tracking.
- [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/) - Affordable rank tracking plus a full SEO suite.
